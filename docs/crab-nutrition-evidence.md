# Cholesterol and vitamins in Shellfie’s crab species

The measurements below describe **100 g of the stated edible sample**. The two Philippine boiled-meat records provide 17.6–19.4 g protein and 0.24–0.52 g saturated fat per 100 g. The blue swimming crab sample also contains 118 mg cholesterol. Evidence for other species often comes from a single region, a specific tissue, or a different preparation. Each result is meaningful on its own sample basis. [DOST-FNRI blue swimming crab, G038](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4018), [DOST-FNRI giant mud crab, G041](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4021)

## Smaller studies and gaps

| Species | Findings and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Orange mud crab (*Scylla olivacea*) | An Indonesian aquaculture trial reported mean meat cholesterol ranging from **90.33 to 100.33 mg/100 g** across groups. Group differences were statistically inconclusive. A separate Bangladesh study measured 19.24 g protein per 100 g raw breast meat from female crabs. Vitamin values remain unverified. [Karim et al., Table 1](https://medwinpublishers.com/IZAB/the-effect-of-different-moon-phases-to-the-dynamics-of-total-cholesterol-and-glycogen-content-of-mangrove-crab-%28scylla-olivacea%29-cultured-with-silvofishery-system.pdf), [Ullah et al., Table 1](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_129349_4ef58862c556eff7599fa576bdcc0bab.pdf#page=4) |
| Crucifix crab (*Charybdis feriata*) | A 1999 Indian study reported **54.2 and 56.5 mg/100 g** under the older name *Charybdis cruciata*. Its authors noted that their method may overestimate cholesterol in crabs because other sterols can contribute to the measurement. A separate Pakistan study reported 15.1 g protein per 100 g wet tissue. Vitamin values remain unverified. [Mathew et al., Table 1(b)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0308-8146%2899%2900050-3), [WoRMS synonym record](https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?id=208820&p=taxdetails), [Umer et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfca.2021.103877) |
| Giant mud crab (*Scylla serrata*) | The same 1999 Indian study reported **51.5 mg/100 g** in claw meat and **54.8 mg/100 g** in its other mud-crab sample, with the same method limitation. These are separate samples from the Philippine boiled-meat record. [Mathew et al., Table 1(b)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0308-8146%2899%2900050-3), [DOST-FNRI G041](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4021) |
| Horn-eyed ghost crab (*Ocypode ceratophthalma*) and Asian shore crab (*Hemigrapsus sanguineus*) | This review found no suitable species-specific measurements for cholesterol or vitamins in edible portions. Use “Not verified” for these values. |
| Mangrove land crab (*Cardisoma carnifex*) | Philippine records document historical food use and a poisoning concern. Keep the risk visible and exclude consumption guidance. [SEAFDEC field guide](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/152/ediblecrustacea.pdf?sequence=1), [SEAFDEC safety handbook](https://repository.seafdec.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.12066/5991/safis25.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) |
| Floral egg crab (*Atergatis floridus*) and devil crab (*Zosimus aeneus*) | **Do not eat, even when cooked.** The Philippine Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources advises against consumption of these two species. [BFAR advisory via the Philippine Information Agency](https://pia.gov.ph/news/beware-of-deadly-crabs-bfar/) |

## Benefits and trade-offs

- **Protein and micronutrients.** The two DOST-FNRI boiled-meat samples are low in saturated fat and provide 279–281 mg calcium per 100 g. Vitamin B2 helps cells use energy from food. Vitamin B12 helps support nerve cells and red blood cell formation. The red frog crab sample’s 3.64 µg B12 is about 152% of the U.S. FDA labeling Daily Value of 2.4 µg. [DOST-FNRI G038](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4018), [DOST-FNRI G041](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4021), [Taiwan FDA J2500201](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/8543), [NIH on vitamin B2](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Riboflavin-HealthProfessional/), [NIH on vitamin B12](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB12-Consumer/), [U.S. FDA Daily Values](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels)
- **Dietary cholesterol and sodium.** The Philippine boiled blue swimming crab sample contains 118 mg cholesterol and 494 mg sodium, about 39% and 21% of the respective U.S. FDA labeling Daily Values per 100 g. These percentages describe the food. They cannot predict someone’s blood LDL response. The American Heart Association’s heart-health guidance emphasizes the overall eating pattern and limiting saturated fat and sodium. Salt and salty sauces add sodium, and butter adds saturated fat. [DOST-FNRI G038](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4018), [U.S. FDA Daily Values](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels), [AHA heart-health dietary guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/healthy-eating/eat-smart/nutrition-basics/aha-diet-and-lifestyle-recommendations), [AHA cooking guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/cholesterol/prevention-and-treatment-of-high-cholesterol-hyperlipidemia/cooking-to-lower-cholesterol)
- **Allergy and food safety.** Crab is a major crustacean shellfish allergen. A photograph or nutrition record cannot establish the safety of the animal someone found. Philippine BFAR and local advisories are the appropriate sources for consumption warnings. [U.S. FDA food allergies](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-allergies-what-you-need-know), [BFAR advisory via PIA](https://pia.gov.ph/news/beware-of-deadly-crabs-bfar/)

For Shellfie’s Nutrition tab, show the portion, preparation, country and source with each measurement. When the app displays a Philippine boiled meat card alongside research from another country, show the foreign measurements as a separate sample. Keep unreported values distinct from measured zeroes and retain the public-health warnings on risky species.
