# Shellfie species and nutrition references

The field guide uses published descriptions and food-composition records. Its species entries remain provisional pending expert review. Reported measurements identify a maximum or a particular specimen, as stated in each entry. The photograph comparison model and its reference catalog have separate coverage.

## Crab information

| Field guide species | Published evidence used |
| --- | --- |
| Giant mud crab (*Scylla serrata*) | The Philippine mangrove manual covers habitat and food use. The taxonomic revision illustrates a 153 mm-wide male. Fazhan et al. describe the pointed frontal lobes, claw spines, and polygon patterns on the limbs. [SEAFDEC/AQD](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/6607/CaballeroPA2025-AEM77.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3), [Keenan et al., Fig. 6](https://era.dpi.qld.gov.au/id/eprint/8476/1/KeenanDavieMann1998_Scyllarevision_lowres.pdf), [Fazhan et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6944125/) |
| Orange mud crab (*Scylla olivacea*) | The same sources cover Philippine habitat, an illustrated 112.7 mm-wide male, low rounded frontal lobes, and limbs without distinct polygon markings. [SEAFDEC/AQD](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/6607/CaballeroPA2025-AEM77.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3), [Keenan et al., Fig. 6](https://era.dpi.qld.gov.au/id/eprint/8476/1/KeenanDavieMann1998_Scyllarevision_lowres.pdf), [Fazhan et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6944125/) |
| Purple mud crab (*Scylla tranquebarica*) | The manual covers Philippine habitat. The revision illustrates a 120.5 mm-wide male. Fazhan et al. describe blunt frontal lobes, purple claws, and polygon markings concentrated on the rear limbs. [SEAFDEC/AQD](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/6607/CaballeroPA2025-AEM77.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3), [Keenan et al., Fig. 6](https://era.dpi.qld.gov.au/id/eprint/8476/1/KeenanDavieMann1998_Scyllarevision_lowres.pdf), [Fazhan et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6944125/) |
| Blue swimming crab (*Portunus pelagicus*) | The revision reports Philippine specimens, a 142 mm-wide reference male, and distinguishing shell proportions and leg segments. SEAFDEC describes coastal habitat and historical Philippine food use under the earlier broader species concept. [Lai et al.](https://treatment.plazi.org/id/AC71483FE167FFDDB747FD53FA68A59E), [SEAFDEC](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/152/ediblecrustacea.pdf?sequence=1#page=78) |
| Three-spot swimming crab (*Portunus sanguinolentus*) | FAO describes the three rear spots, side teeth, and habitat down to about 30 m. SEAFDEC records Philippine food use. [FAO species account](https://isopods.nhm.org/pdfs/4113/4113.pdf#page=88), [SEAFDEC](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/152/ediblecrustacea.pdf?sequence=1#page=80) |
| Crucifix crab (*Charybdis feriata*) | FAO describes the cross-like shell pattern, pale leg spots, and sandy-mud habitat. SEAFDEC records Philippine food use. Both use the older spelling *C. feriatus*. [FAO species account](https://isopods.nhm.org/pdfs/4113/4113.pdf#page=83), [SEAFDEC](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/152/ediblecrustacea.pdf?sequence=1#page=82) |
| Red frog crab (*Ranina ranina*) | FAO describes the elongated shell, digging legs, sandy seabeds, and reported maximum shell lengths. SEAFDEC records the species in Philippine waters including Zamboanga. [FAO species account](https://isopods.nhm.org/pdfs/4113/4113.pdf#page=53), [SEAFDEC](https://repository.seafdec.org.ph/bitstream/handle/10862/152/ediblecrustacea.pdf?sequence=1#page=72) |
| Mangrove land crab (*Cardisoma carnifex*) | FAO describes the inflated shell, enlarged male claw, coastal burrows, and reported food use. SEAFDEC documents a Philippine poisoning hazard. [FAO species account](https://isopods.nhm.org/pdfs/4113/4113.pdf#page=112), [SEAFDEC safety handbook](https://repository.seafdec.org/handle/20.500.12066/5991) |
| Horn-eyed ghost crab (*Ocypode ceratophthalma*) | FAO describes the square shell, adult eyestalk extensions, sandy upper-beach burrows, and the comparison with *O. cordimanus*. [FAO species account](https://isopods.nhm.org/pdfs/4113/4113.pdf#page=116) |
| Asian shore crab (*Hemigrapsus sanguineus*) | The Australian Government describes the square shell, three teeth per side, and banded legs. USGS describes the northwest Pacific native range and rocky habitat. These references leave Philippine occurrence unverified. [Australian Government](https://www.marinepests.gov.au/pests/identify/asian-shore-crab), [USGS](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/factsheet.aspx?SpeciesID=183) |
| Devil crab (*Zosimus aeneus*) | Naturalis describes the oval, bumpy shell and two measured specimens. BFAR identifies the species in its warning against consumption, including after cooking. [Naturalis](https://crabs-japan.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/species/taxon.php?id=33964), [BFAR advisory via the Philippine Information Agency](https://pia.gov.ph/news/beware-of-deadly-crabs-bfar/) |
| Floral egg crab (*Atergatis floridus*) | The museum account describes the rounded shell and lace-like pattern. Naturalis lists a Cebu record. BFAR identifies the species in its warning against consumption, including after cooking. [Western Australian Museum](https://museum.wa.gov.au/online-collections/names/atergatis-floridus/), [Naturalis](https://crabs-japan.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/species/taxon.php?id=33976), [BFAR advisory via the Philippine Information Agency](https://pia.gov.ph/news/beware-of-deadly-crabs-bfar/) |

## Crab nutrition

Values are per 100 g of the stated sample. Where a source did not report cholesterol, the app shows “Not reported.” The FDA Daily Value is used only for records with a measured cholesterol value. [FDA Daily Value guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/daily-value-nutrition-and-supplement-facts-labels)

| Species | Sample and selected results |
| --- | --- |
| Blue swimming crab | Boiled without crab fat, 98 kcal, 19.4 g protein, 0.9 g fat, and 118 mg cholesterol. [DOST-FNRI G038](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4018) |
| Giant mud crab | Boiled without crab fat, 88 kcal, 17.6 g protein, and 1.9 g fat. [DOST-FNRI G041](https://i.fnri.dost.gov.ph/fct/library/report/4021) |
| Orange mud crab | Raw meat from the breast of females sampled in Bangladesh, 19.24 g protein, 1.46 g fat, and 2.89 g carbohydrate. [Ullah et al., Table 1](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_129349_4ef58862c556eff7599fa576bdcc0bab.pdf#page=4) |
| Purple mud crab | Raw male body meat from India, 15.63 g protein and 0.65 g fat. [Sreelakshmi et al., Table 2](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJF/article/download/34511/24689/488419#page=2) |
| Three-spot swimming crab | Raw male meat from Bangladesh, 14.02 g protein, 2.78 g fat, and 220.71 mg sodium. [Al Mamun et al., Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jafr.2024.100972) |
| Crucifix crab | Crab meat from Pakistan on a wet-weight basis, 65.5 kcal and 15.1 g protein. [Umer et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfca.2021.103877) |
| Red frog crab | Raw edible parts from sample J2500201, 72 kcal, 16.6 g protein, 0.1 g fat, and 54 mg cholesterol. The Taiwan Ministry of Agriculture identifies this database food name as *Ranina ranina*. [Taiwan FDA dataset](https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/8543), [Taiwan Ministry of Agriculture](https://kmweb.moa.gov.tw/theme_data.php?id=155&theme=fisheasy) |

No suitable food-composition record was found for the Asian shore crab or horn-eyed ghost crab. The mangrove land crab entry shows the documented poisoning risk. The devil crab and floral egg crab entries show the BFAR warning. [SEAFDEC safety handbook](https://repository.seafdec.org/handle/20.500.12066/5991), [BFAR advisory via the Philippine Information Agency](https://pia.gov.ph/news/beware-of-deadly-crabs-bfar/)
